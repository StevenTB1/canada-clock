## Character

**Partial capture:** the page is a single 900px screen that scrolls inside a canvas, so only the first screen was measured (the cookie dialog was removed before measuring). A warm off-white ground (`#edeee5`) carries one enormous wordmark: greedBold at 293px with a 0.75 line height, cropped by the bottom edge. A strip of full-bleed project photos across the top supplies all the colour, with a pale yellow (`#fdf48b`) as the only brand accent. Everything else is small: 12px uppercase PP Neue Montreal labels and an 18px intro paragraph, on a tight 8px spacing step. The contrast between one giant word and tiny utility text is the whole idea.
