## Character

Full capture. The most literal black-and-white site of the set: white and light grey (`#ffffff`, `#f1f1f1`) with black and one grey (`#8a8a8a`) for text, and no accent colour at all. The page is a directory, so after a giant wordmark and a three-card article row it becomes one long table: numbered rows, section labels, hairline dividers, names left and URLs right. Helvetica Neue at about 16px for names and descriptions, Space Mono for metadata, links and headlines (24px, −0.03em). Spacing is dense and regular on an 8/16px step, with small 7px radii on cards. Colour comes only from article thumbnails and one large photo.
