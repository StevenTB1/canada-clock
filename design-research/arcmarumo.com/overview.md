## Character

**Partial capture:** one 900px screen rendered as an old CRT monitor through WebGL; later screens were not reached. Near-black ground (`#111111` over `#050505`) with all text in a 13.75px uppercase monospace at weight 500 and −0.035em tracking, in warm off-whites (`#e4dfd9`, `#f6f5ee`) rather than pure white. Text sits only at the four corners and bottom centre, like a camera viewfinder readout (timecode, source, B&W toggle), leaving the middle empty for the work. The only colour is the small rainbow stripe in the logo. Slow 0.9s ease-out transitions match the analogue mood.
