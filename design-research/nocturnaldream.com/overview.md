## Character

**Partial capture:** one 900px screen; the experience continues inside WebGL canvases that the probe did not reach. Near-black ground (`#020203`) with a faint pattern of overlapping circles, and a parchment cream (`#f1e8d0`) used for both text and the highlight boxes behind the headline. The headline is a condensed black display face (PP Formula Condensed) set as stacked cream labels with yellow marker strokes scribbled over words. Everything else is tiny 7.5–10px uppercase PP Supply Mono. Warm cream instead of white is what keeps it from feeling cold.
